## Verdict (orchestrator, 10 Nov 2026 UTC+8)

- **P1 PASS:** gate_r 85.35 vs max(gate 79.63, ffr 78.24) + 1.0 = 80.63 (+5.7). The exact-match store over the replaying
  cortex is the best route that has a cortex in it.
- **P2 FAIL → deletion by "right twice" killed:** gate_rd 84.24 vs 84.35 needed (−0.11), and the store kept 84% of its
  entries (bar ≤ 50%).

Mechanism: "right on its last 2 exposures" can only fire for items that repeat. Normal MNIST arrivals essentially never
repeat, so they are never deleted, and they are ~80% of the store. Meanwhile some episodes were deleted after two lucky
or transient cortex hits and then missed (6+ exposures: 100 → 89.6). Deletion after consolidation is the wrong end:
the history-compressor rule is to **not write what the cortex already predicts**. Next: H24d, surprise-gated writes.

Note: ffr lifetime differs from H24a by +0.59 / +0.10 / −0.27 points (replay-sampling nondeterminism); within seed noise.
Caveat (as in H24a): in this short life the exemplar store alone (87.3) beats every route on normal digits too, because
the cortex is undertrained at 10 000 ticks.

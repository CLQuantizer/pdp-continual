Protocol notes:
- World: FactWorld, channels G C1 C7 E30 E110 M7 M37 F N Z. Glance = 64 symbols.
- N: uniform over 42 symbols. Z: the space symbol repeated. Seeds 15 16 17 18 19 20.
- GRU: symbol embedding 32, channel embedding 8, hidden 256, Adam lr 3e-3, clip 1.0.
  Per-channel state. One live Adam step per glance, then `dose` replay steps.
- Controller rand. No utility store and no match, so W is the frozen GRU on a fresh
  2048-symbol continuation of each learnable channel.
- hist* calls m4b.sample_history: uniform over starts 0 through L−64 on every channel
  with L ≥ 64. hist2 is that machine at dose 2, the same dose as M4c hist_rep.
- res* is Algorithm R over the glance segments seen so far, capacity fixed at 77 or 16
  (77 = 15% of the T = 512 log; 16 ≈ 3%). fifo77 drops the oldest glance past 77.
- Each replay step draws 8 segments with replacement. Traffic = dose × 8.
- M = segments × 64 × log2(42). hist* counts every observed glance. nostore counts 0.
- Reservoir replacement uses numpy seed 40_000 + seed. Replay draws use 30_000 + seed.
  Choice and observations use 10_000 + seed, shared by every condition.
- First pass: glance g visits channel g for g < 10, then the controller.
- The raw log stays in the run so glances can be indexed. For res* and fifo77 it is
  not the replay source; M does not charge it.

## Verdict (orchestrator, 10 Nov 2026, UTC+8)

**Both bars FAIL, and the failure is informative: a small buffer replayed hard overfits. What the bus must carry is
*distinct* past data, few times each; less replay is better than more.**

1. **Fair bounded sample suffices: FAIL.** res77 − hist2 = **+0.386** (bar ≤ +0.03). res77 (1.877) is even *worse
   than no replay* (1.808) on 5/6 seeds; res16 is far worse (2.251).
2. **Fairness beats recency: FAIL (reversed).** res77 − fifo77 = +0.086; FIFO ≈ no replay.
3. **Dose curve:** hist1 1.437 (best) < hist2 1.491 < hist4 1.636; bits saved per unit traffic 0.046 / 0.020 / 0.005.
   Past one batched step per glance, more traffic *hurts*.

**Mechanism (reading, consistent with every row).** At 2 steps × 8 segments per glance, a 77-segment buffer replays
each stored segment ~100 times over a life and the GRU memorises it; full-history replay draws from ~32k distinct
offsets, so each window is seen a handful of times. The value of replay scales with distinct data per repeat, not
with traffic. This is the H8/every-38 knee again, now in the active world, and it settles the note's claim for this
learner: **a fixed small state cannot replace the log for learning; the log (or a large fraction of it) is what makes
replay useful, and the bus should run lighter (1 step, not 2).** Open: does a bounded buffer help at a replay rate
matched to its size (few repeats per stored symbol)? That is H27b.

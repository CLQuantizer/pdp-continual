**Rule from H27 + H27b:** replay works while each stored segment is replayed up to ~10 times (res308_e1: 8.8 → fine);
at ~100 the cortex memorises the buffer and replay hurts (H27 res77). So the memory a replaying machine needs scales
with traffic × life / (repeats allowed): bounded at any fixed life length, but not a constant over an unbounded
life unless the replay rate falls with age. P / M / traffic: full log (P 1.457, M 1.0, traffic 8) is dominated by
the 60% reservoir (P 1.428, M 0.6, traffic 8).

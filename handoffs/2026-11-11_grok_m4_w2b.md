# M4-W2b: visit-to-visit learning progress, and exploration off the reset button (grok, Kaggle CPU)

Read `/root/pdp/CLAUDE.md` first, then `results/m4w2/RESULTS.md` (including its Verdict), `m4w2.py`,
`w2_world.py`, `w2_headroom.py` and `h25d_visit_slope.py`.

## Context
M4-W2 failed, for three reasons:
- **In-sample progress rewarded memorising the TV.** The reward re-scored the chunk the learner had just been
  trained on. The TV earned about 0.035 bits per step and the learned rooms about 0.003.
- **ε-exploration included reset.** That is a spec error: with ε = 0.1, a random reset comes every 80 steps, and
  opening a lock takes about 216. So no arm could get past room 2.
- **Room-only state** cannot represent lock progress.

M4-W2b fixes the first two and keeps the third. With exploration off reset, random manipulations open locks the way
rand6 does. The controller's real decisions are then whether to watch the TV and when to reset. Principles served:
reward compression progress (3) and act to maximise it (4).

## What changes from `m4w2.py` (everything else is identical)
Create `m4w2b.py`. Import from `m4w2.py`, `w2_world.py` and `w2_headroom.py`, and do not edit them.

1. **ε-exploration over manipulations only.** With probability ε = 0.1, pick uniformly from actions {0..5}. Otherwise
   take argmax Q[room] over all 8 actions, with ties broken uniformly. Greedy may still choose the TV or reset. This
   applies to every `_q` / `_lp` arm.
2. **New reward, `prog_lp`: visit-to-visit learning progress (Oudeyer-style, prequential).** The context is
   c = (room before the action, action). For each context, keep a list of the prequential `bits_before` of its
   observation at each visit. These are the live scores before any update on that chunk; nothing is re-scored. With
   K = 16:
   - At visit n ≤ 2K of context c, the reward is 1.0 (optimistic: an untried context is worth trying).
   - At visit n > 2K, the reward is mean(bits at visits n−2K … n−K−1) − mean(bits at visits n−K … n−1). This is the
     drop in this context's loss between the older window and the recent window. The current visit's own bits are
     *not* included, because it has not been predicted yet when the action is chosen. Its bits join the list after
     the chunk is scored.
   - Rewards are computed when the controller is updated after each chunk, transition by transition in order.
     Within a chunk, a visit's reward uses only bits from earlier visits.
   - On the TV, the loss stays about 4 bits, so the expected reward is 0. On a room being learned it is positive. On a
     learned room it is about 0.
3. **No re-score pass** is needed for `prog_lp`. Keep it only for the `prog_q` arm.

Q-learning (α = 0.1, γ = 0.9, 9 × 8 table, zero-initialised, state = room), the controller rng
`default_rng(7_000 + world_seed)`, the learner, replay, probe and metrics are all as in M4-W2.

## Arms and seeds
- Five arms: `rand6`, `prog_lp` (new reward), `prog_q` (M4-W2's in-sample reward, with the new ε; this isolates the
  estimator), `count_q` (new ε) and `err_q` (new ε).
- **World seeds 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.** These are fresh and held out. Seeds 3–8 were used to diagnose M4-W2.
- That makes 30 lives. Use three private CPU kernels, `ezzzzzx/m4w2b-a`, `-b` and `-c`, with seeds (9, 10), (11, 12)
  and (13, 14). Each has `"is_private": true`, `"enable_gpu": false` and `"enable_internet": false`. The code goes up as
  private dataset `ezzzzzx/pdp-code-m4w2b`. No GPU.

## Smoke test (VPS CPU, before any kernel)
1. **Equivalence:** with world seed 0 and T = 640, `rand6` through `m4w2b.py` gives the same token sha256 and total bits
   as M4-W2's `rand6`, to 1e-6.
2. **ε check:** over a 2,000-step `count_q` run where Q is forced to all zeros and never updated, every reset and TV
   press must come from a greedy tie-break, never from the ε branch. Count actions by branch and report the counts.
3. **prog_lp reward check, on a synthetic list:** for a context whose bits are 4.0 at every visit, the reward after
   2K visits is exactly 0. For bits that fall linearly from 4 to 0 over visits 1..64, the reward at visit 40 is
   positive and equals the hand-computed value. Report both.
4. Each arm with T = 640 gives finite bits and finite Q.
If any check fails, stop and report. Do not push kernels.

## Outputs
`results/m4w2b/RESULTS.md` and `results/m4w2b/results.json`, with numbers only and no verdict. They use the same
fields as M4-W2, plus:
- the gaps per seed, with mean ± std (ddof = 0), on PROBE_cap: rand6 − prog_lp, count_q − prog_lp, prog_q − prog_lp,
  err_q − prog_lp;
- the TV and reset shares per arm and per quarter;
- for `prog_lp`, the mean reward per (room, a) over the last quarter and the final Q table.

## Pre-registered bars (orchestrator scores; do not change)
For scale, the planning headroom (rand6 − oracle, PROBE_cap) was 0.50 ± 0.14 on seeds 3–8.
1. **Curiosity beats the no-reset random walk:** mean over seeds 9–14 of (rand6 − prog_lp) on PROBE_cap is ≥ 0.25, and
   prog_lp beats rand6 on at least 4 of the 6 seeds.
2. **Progress beats novelty:** mean (count_q − prog_lp) ≥ 0.10.
3. **Progress ignores the TV:** prog_lp puts ≤ 10% of its steps on the TV.
Report only: prog_q − prog_lp, the effect of the estimator alone.

Kill rule: if prog_lp is worse than rand6 on mean PROBE_cap, curiosity with a room-only tabular controller does not
pay in W2. The next step is then a controller with memory (state that includes the recent action and output), not
another reward.

# M4-W2d: curiosity in W2 with reset taken only on purpose (grok, Kaggle CPU)

Read `/root/pdp/CLAUDE.md` first, then `results/m4w2c/RESULTS.md` (including its Verdict), `m4w2c.py`, `m4w2b.py`,
`m4w2.py`, `w2_world.py` and `w2_headroom.py`.

## Context
Three runs of a curious controller in W2 failed, and all three for the same reason, which was a spec error: the
controller pressed reset at random. In M4-W2 that came through ε; in M4-W2b and M4-W2c it came through greedy
tie-breaks over all 8 actions, so every fresh state pressed reset one time in eight. That is rand8, which never gets
past room 2. The capability check in M4-W2c showed that even a perfect reward could not get the memory controller past
room 1.

M4-W2d removes the error and changes nothing else: reset (action 7) and the TV (action 6) are taken only when their Q
is strictly the highest. They are never chosen at random. This is the cheapest fair test of the core claim: progress
as a reward drives an agent deeper into a world where its actions matter. Principles served: reward compression
progress (3) and act to maximise it (4).

## What changes from `m4w2c.py` (everything else identical)
Create `m4w2d.py`. Import from `m4w2c.py`, `m4w2b.py`, `m4w2.py`, `w2_world.py` and `w2_headroom.py`, and do not edit
them.

**The action rule, for every learning arm:** with probability ε = 0.1, pick uniformly from manipulations {0..5}.
Otherwise let m = max over all 8 actions of Q(s, a).
- If some action in {6, 7} has Q = m and no manipulation in {0..5} has Q = m, take that action. If both 6 and 7 do,
  take the lower id.
- Otherwise pick uniformly among the manipulations in {0..5} with Q = m.

So reset and the TV are taken only when the controller has learned that they are strictly best. Use the controller rng
`default_rng(7_000 + world_seed)`.

Everything else is as in M4-W2c: the state, α = 0.1, γ = 0.9, the rewards, the learner, the chunks and the scoring.

## Arms and seeds
- Arms: `rand6`, `lp_mem`, `count_mem` and `lp_room`, defined as in M4-W2c.
- **World seeds 21–26**, which are fresh and held out (seeds 3–20 have been used).
- That makes 24 lives. Use three private CPU kernels, `ezzzzzx/m4w2d-a`, `-b` and `-c`, with seeds (21, 22), (23, 24)

  private dataset `ezzzzzx/pdp-code-m4w2d`. No GPU.

## Smoke test (VPS CPU, before any kernel)
1. **Equivalence:** with world seed 0 and T = 640, `rand6` through `m4w2d.py` matches M4-W2c's `rand6` exactly: the
   same token sha256, and total bits to 1e-6.
2. **Rule check:** on hand-made Q rows, print the chosen action over 1,000 draws each, for these rows:
   - all zeros (expect only 0..5, about uniform);
   - Q[7] = 0.5 with the rest 0 (expect 7 whenever greedy);
   - Q[7] = Q[2] = 0.5 (expect 2 whenever greedy, never 7).
3. **Capability check:** repeat M4-W2c's check (fixed reward +1 for entering a never-entered room, memory state,
   20,000 steps, world seed 0) with the new rule. Report the deepest room and the first step it was reached, next to
   M4-W2c's result (room 1).
4. Each arm with T = 640 gives finite bits and finite Q.
If check 1 or 2 fails, stop and report. Do not push kernels.

## Pre-registered bars (orchestrator scores; do not change)
For scale: rand6 − oracle on PROBE_cap was 0.50 ± 0.14 (seeds 3–8); rand6 alone was 1.13 ± 0.42 on seeds 15–20.
1. **Curiosity beats the random walk:** mean over seeds 21–26 of (rand6 − lp_mem) on PROBE_cap is ≥ 0.25, and lp_mem
   beats rand6 on at least 4 of the 6 seeds.
2. **Progress beats novelty:** mean (count_mem − lp_mem) ≥ 0.10.
Report only: lp_room − lp_mem; TV share of lp_mem, which should stay ≤ 0.10; deepest room per arm.

Kill rule: if the capability check stays at room 2 or below, the tabular controller cannot open locks even with a
perfect reward. Then record bars 1 and 2 as void, not failed, and the next design is the model-based planner.
